  • Günlük Yaşam Takip Günlükleri: Su tüketim miktarları (ml), uyku süreleri ve saatleri, yürüyüş ve egzersiz dakikaları, stres seviyesi (1-5), sigara kullanım sayısı, meyve/sebze tüketim durumu. (Gelişim raporlarınızı grafiksel olarak sunmak için).
  • Uygulama İçi Tercihler: Seçili arayüz dili (Türkçe, İngilizce, Almanca, Rusça), karanlık mod tercihi ve kazanılan rozet/başarı bilgileri.

Application Name: Heart & Lifestyle Tracker (Kalp ve Yaşam: Sağlık Takibi)

Package ID: com.vistra.cardioguard

Developer Team: Heart & Lifestyle Development Team

Summary Statement: "Heart & Lifestyle Tracker" (com.vistra.cardioguard) is built with a 100% offline-first architecture. All your personal profile parameters and health logs are stored strictly on your device's internal SQLite (Room) database and are never transmitted to external servers.

1. Processed Data Categories & Purposes

Data stored in the app is processed locally solely to deliver core fitness and wellness features:

  • Physical Profile Data: Age, gender, height, weight. (Used to compute Body Mass Index (BMI) and daily hydration/sleep targets).
  • Clinical Risk Parameters: Blood pressure category, cholesterol level, diabetes status, and family history of heart disease. (Used to compute overall heart health awareness scores).
  • Daily Lifestyle Logs: Water consumption (ml), sleep duration and bedtime/waketime, walking and exercise minutes, stress level (1-5), cigarette counts, fruit/vegetable intake. (Used to generate historical wellness charts).
  • User Preferences: Selected language (English, Turkish, German, Russian), dark mode toggle, and unlocked achievements/badges.

2. DATA RETENTION POLICY

Data Retention Notice: Our application DOES NOT STORE OR RETAIN any user data on external servers or cloud databases.

In accordance with Google Play User Data Policies, our data retention principles are as follows:

  • No Cloud or External Server Retention: Our app does not require user accounts, email registration, or cloud backup. No user data is ever collected, stored, or retained on external servers or cloud services.
  • Local Retention Duration: Information entered into the app is retained exclusively inside the protected SQLite (Room DB) Android sandbox on your device for as long as the application remains installed.

3. DATA DELETION METHODS AND USER RIGHTS (Data Deletion Policy)

Users maintain complete 100% ownership and control over data deletion:

  • In-App Data Deletion: You can purge all database tables (`user_profile`, `daily_logs`, `water_logs`, `sleep_logs`, `activity_logs`, `smoking_logs`, `achievements`) permanently at any time by tapping Settings -> Reset Profile / Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation Data Deletion: Uninstalling the application automatically prompts the Android OS to permanently wipe out all local database files and internal app storage. Because no data is stored on remote servers, no residual data remains anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

Our app incorporates Google Play Services and Google AdMob to display advertisements:

  • AdMob may process anonymous advertising identifiers (Ad-ID) in compliance with Google Advertising Policies.
  • Strict Guarantee: Your health records, profile stats, and heart risk score data are NEVER shared with advertising networks or used for targeted ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age and does not collect data from children.

6. Medical Disclaimer

This app is designed for educational awareness and lifestyle tracking. It is not a certified medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any cardiovascular diseases.
