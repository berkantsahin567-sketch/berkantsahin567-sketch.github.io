Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2025

1. Introduction

CardioGuard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

Name, email, date of birth, gender, device identifiers, IP address, and usage data.

2.2 Health Data

ECG records, heart rate, AI analysis results. Health data is classified as PHI under HIPAA and Special Category Data under GDPR.

3. How We Use Your Information

To provide, maintain, and improve the Service, perform AI analysis via MedGemma, send alerts, and generate reports.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored locally in encrypted SQLite and synced to cloud using TLS 1.3 if enabled. AES-256 encryption is used.

5. HIPAA Compliance

We comply with HIPAA standards. Business Associate Agreements (BAA) are available.

6. GDPR Rights

Rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, portability, and object. Contact: privacy@cardioguard.ai

7. Data Retention

Retained while account is active. Deleted from cloud within 305 days of account deletion.
